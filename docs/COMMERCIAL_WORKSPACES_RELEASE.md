# BARUNA Sentinel — Commercial Workspace Shell (Bhumireka 2026)
Date: 16 November 2026

## Product outcome
Commercial presentation is a **working project portfolio and consistent navigation**, not fabricated new customers or unsafe risk scores.

### Workspaces
- **Workspaces / `workspaces.html`**: Portfolio switcher with three real entry points. All cards link to existing, functional views.
- **Tanjung Emas / `?page=assessment`**: One source-backed research case (six historical 2005 sectors); no current asset-level ranking permitted.
- **Spatial Decision Lab / `spatial-demo.html`**: Four fictional asset polygons. A/B/C ranked within scenario policy; D excluded because evidence gate fails.
- **GNSS Evidence Lab / `?page=analyst`**: Historical CSEM GPS positions and independent BAKO RINEX QC. Not additional customer infrastructure projects.

### Presentation changes
- Introduced a clean professional portfolio screen with persistent sidebar, clear workspace titles and source-state labels.
- Replaced the competition-style introductory billboard in Spatial Demo with compact project context, map-centric workspace, three measured demo counts, and tab navigation.
- Retained the explicit Demo Scenario label in-context instead of a full-width banner.
- Added a clear Workspaces entry to the main source-backed interface.
- Kept the diagrammatic geography clearly illustrative, rather than official asset footprints.
- Original evidence/ranking policies, output and fixtures were NOT changed.

### Conformity to original RGPD proposal
The proposal (BARUNA Sentinel RGPD 2026, submitted original) calls for:
- multi-GNSS evidence, quality checking, movement interpretation and **spatial association**;
- a map, inspection priority list, evidence card and short verification report;
- explicit source provenance, coverage and uncertainty;
- differentiated real evidence versus illustrative geographic scenario;
- **screening** and inspection support, not certified structural safety.

This workspace structure supports those needs while keeping the future portfolio monitoring ambition as a product direction, not a falsely delivered feature.

### Review and freeze conditions
- Unit/scientific checks, offline bundle and desktop/mobile browser journey must pass.
- Hosted deployment must be independently verified; green CI does not prove production deployed.
- This remains a **frontend-only case/portfolio shell**. It does not provide persisted multi-project tenancy, authenticated access, synchronized field assets, operator contracts or additional official measurements.
