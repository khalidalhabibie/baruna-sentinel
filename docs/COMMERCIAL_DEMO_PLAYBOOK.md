# BARUNA Sentinel — buyer-facing demo playbook
**Ourdo Indonesia · BIG Bhumireka 2026 · Semarang evidence case**

## The offer, in plain language

**Know what to verify before the next survey.**

BARUNA Sentinel helps engineering, geodetic and asset-maintenance teams turn disconnected historical evidence into a traceable **field verification request**. It shows what is documented, what is not established, and what new evidence the responsible team should request.

This competition case is **not** operational asset monitoring, a current coastal alert, an InSAR-derived asset-risk map, a structural safety assessment, or a paid customer deployment.

## One-screen product journey

| Step | Viewer sees | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Select a sector | Six published historical port-sector records from a 2005 field study | The demo uses authentic, source-labelled case data |
| 2. Review the decision | Historic cumulative settlement, missing current on-asset data, three evidence requests | Missing evidence does not become an invented risk score |
| 3. Export verification brief | Sector-specific print / Save PDF | An operationally intelligible handoff, not just a map |
| Optional | Location context disclosure | Verified public reference points only; exact surveyed-sector polygons are unavailable |
| Technical | GNSS Evidence Lab | CSEM historic positions and independent BAKO observational QC are auditable and not conflated |

## Buyer and commercial hypothesis

**Primary buyer hypothesis:** operator, maintenance / asset engineering team, or geodetic consultancy that must consolidate historic reference records and justify follow-up field work.

**First sellable experiment:** scoped *Evidence Readiness & Survey Planning Assessment*. Deliver the evidence inventory, geometry/CRS/provenance audit, gaps register, sector-level verification requests, and a review with a qualified domain professional. Price discovery should happen in customer interviews; this is not an established rate card.

**Not a SaaS subscription claim yet.** Repeat usage, budget owner, business case, security requirements, field-data ownership, responsibility for measurements, and procurement are all unvalidated.

**Qualification questions:** (1) When did you last have to choose where or what to survey? (2) What sources did you combine? (3) How long did preparation and review actually take? (4) What errors or revisions were common? (5) Who owns the budget for resolving the workflow?

**GO only after evidence:** a relevant team shares a real workflow and permitted sample records, an independent geodesy/engineering reviewer validates the method, and at least one buyer expresses a measurable willingness to pay (ideally a paid scoped pilot).

## Scientific and legal gate

- The 2005 port field study reports historical cumulative settlement for six sectors; the demo has no exact surveyed polygon geometry or current on-asset readings.
- CSEM 2010–2021 GPS-derived station positions are **regional station-level historical evidence**, not movement of an identified port structure.
- BAKO 2025 verified RINEX observation QC is independent and cannot validate CSEM station movement, prove improved positioning accuracy or establish port-asset deformation.
- Public OSM references are not authoritative asset geometry. Do not fabricate asset pins, hazard rasters, predicted flood levels, annual rates from cumulative figures, safety status, or financial savings.
- Ina-CORS / SRGI data redistribution may require approval; keep local restricted QC local until terms are checked.
- GNSS observation presence alone is not an engineering structural safety conclusion.

## Research and reference patterns reviewed

- [ArcGIS Field Maps — inspections and related asset reports](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-field-maps/latest/use-maps/inspect.html): select an existing record and attach inspection history.
- [ArcGIS Solutions — conduct field inspections](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/arcgis-solutions/latest/app/build-your-own-solution-using-the-arcgis-solutions-assistant.htm): work orders, field inventory and inspection result monitoring.
- [Felt interface tour](https://help.felt.com/getting-started/tour-the-interface): contextual layer, detail and table panels; progressively exposed map tools.
- [SkyGeo — infrastructure monitoring](https://www.skygeo.com/insar-for-civil-engineering-infrastructure): quality, uncertainty, historical context and site-specific specialist review before action.

## What to show vs. not show

**Show:** source-backed sector selection → missing-evidence decision → verification PDF → short independent GNSS proof.

**Avoid:** lengthy historical background, animated landing-page hero, invented red/green risk scores, large map as the product, private station QC unless permitted, or a claim of commercial deployment.

**One real next action:** meet at least one infrastructure or survey practitioner during the event, show the verification brief, and ask for a sample of their existing evidence-review workflow. Finish this discovery before adding more layers.
