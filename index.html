What does the comparison prove?

The plot compares time coverage and GNSS pseudorange observation presence, not millimetre positioning accuracy. Missing files do not prove a receiver outage. CSEM (historic GPS-derived displacement), CSMG and the independent BAKO multi-GNSS QC are not interchangeable station or time series.
