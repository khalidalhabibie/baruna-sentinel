What do these data mean?

Historical settlement: published cumulative measurements in 2005, not 2026 asset conditions or a yearly rate.

CSEM GPS: historic station coordinates from 2010–2021. A regional station is not an instrument on the selected port structure.

BAKO multi-GNSS: authentic observation-quality data from Cibinong in 2025, not a Semarang movement calculation.

WGS84 / EPSG:4326: coordinates and approximate distances provide geographic context, not a coastal exposure or engineering hazard model.
